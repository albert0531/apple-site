Did You Know?

A handful of Apple facts worth repeating.

Apple is older than the personal computer industry.

Founded April 1, 1976 — before the Apple II, before Microsoft moved to Albuquerque, before the word "PC" meant anything.

Steve Wozniak designed the Apple I by hand.

The whole machine was a single circuit board Woz handed out at the Homebrew Computer Club.

The iPhone was almost "Purple."

Apple considered names including Purple and Telipod. "iPhone" only narrowly beat them out internally.

Apple almost went bankrupt in 1997.

90 days from insolvency before Microsoft invested $150M — a bailout arranged by Steve Jobs after his return.

The "Apple" name came from a fruitarian diet.

Jobs was into fruitarian diets at the time and liked the sound. The logo came later.

M-series chips started on iPad, not Mac.

The A4 in 2010 was Apple's first in-house SoC. M1 in 2020 finished the transition: Macs running iPad silicon.

Apple Intelligence

The new era of AI on Apple devices.

Apple Intelligence & New Siri

What Apple unveiled at WWDC 2026, on-device and in the cloud.

On-device foundation models

Apple's next-gen LLM runs locally on A19 Pro and M5-class chips. Summaries, Writing Tools, Image Playground, Genmoji — all without leaving your device.

Private Cloud Compute

Heavier requests route to a custom Apple Silicon server. Apple can't see your data — the architecture is publicly audited.

New Siri (2026)

Conversational, on-screen aware, and able to act across apps. "Send the file Eric sent me last week to Mom" — actually works now, on-device when possible.

Visual Intelligence

Long-press the camera or a photo to search, translate, summarize, or shop. Now understands context across apps and the screen you're looking at.

Image Playground + Genmoji

Generate images in styles like Sketch, Illustration, and Animation. Genmoji builds custom emoji from your prompt, on-device.

Writing Tools, leveled up

Rewrite, proofread, summarize — across Mail, Notes, Pages, Safari, and third-party apps via the standard API.

ChatGPT integration

Optional handoff to ChatGPT for queries Siri can't answer locally. Free, no account needed, privacy prompts before any data leaves.

Developer APIs

App Intents, the Foundation Model framework, and Image Playground API — third-party apps can plug into Apple Intelligence directly.

History

Five decades, one company.

1976

Founded in a garage

Steve Jobs, Steve Wozniak, and Ronald Wayne sell the Apple I for $666.66.

1977

Apple II ships

One of the first successful personal computers. Color graphics, expandable.

1984

Macintosh

GUI for the masses. The "1984" Super Bowl ad airs once and rewrites ad history.

1985

Jobs leaves Apple

Board-level ousting. He goes on to found NeXT and buy Pixar.

1997

Jobs returns

Apple acquires NeXT; Jobs back as interim CEO. The Microsoft investment follows.

2001

iPod & iTunes

"1,000 songs in your pocket." Apple reshapes music distribution.

2007

iPhone

"An iPod, a phone, an internet communicator." Three products, one device.

2010

iPad

Apple defines a new category between phone and laptop.

2011

Tim Cook becomes CEO

Jobs passes away August 24. Cook takes over; the company more than triples in value under his tenure.

2020

Apple Silicon on Mac

M1 launches. Macs leave Intel behind. iPhone-class efficiency, laptop-class performance.

2024

Apple Intelligence & Vision Pro

Spatial computing ships. On-device LLMs land in iOS, iPadOS, macOS.
